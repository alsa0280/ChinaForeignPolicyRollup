TABLE: ai_claims
Purpose: Stores the AI's validated analysis of each article (scores, summary and extracted claims) as an auditable record.
Implementation: n8n data table "ai_claims" (created by setup_tables; written by the "Insert AI Claim" node after the "Verify AI Claims" Code node).

Columns
  source_id                   text    source the article came from
  url                         text    article URL
  hash                        text    hash of the article (links back to documents)
  credibility_score           number  1-5, how reliable the article seems (clamped to 1-5)
  relevance_score             number  1-5, relevance to Canada-China commercial opportunities
  relevance_justification_en  text    short English reason for the relevance score
  summary_en                  text    English summary
  claims_json                 text    JSON array of validated claims (see event for the fields)
  claims_dropped              number  how many AI claims were rejected by validation
  analyzed_at                 date    when the AI result was stored

Rules
  - One row per document (1:1 with documents through "hash").
  - A claim is kept only if its opportunity_id is one of the 28 valid IDs and
    its support_quote appears word-for-word in the article.
  - Claims are AI-extracted and unreviewed.
